# Privacy

DSH-Portable does not operate a telemetry or analytics service. The Portable shell disables DSH telemetry by default, and sessions, settings, credentials, plugins, logs, and workspaces stay in the product's data directory unless you choose to share or move them.

The application connects to external services only when a feature needs them:

- model requests go to the provider you configure;
- update checks contact GitHub when you run a manual check or enable startup checks;
- the plugin market contacts its listed public sources when you open or refresh it;
- links you open follow the privacy terms of their destination.

Support reports are created locally and exclude known credentials. They are not uploaded automatically. Review any report before sharing it in an issue or discussion.

DSH-Portable is an independent community distribution. The privacy behavior of DeepSeek Harness, model providers, and third-party plugins remains governed by those projects and services.
